## Compensation policy (floor = signal, not contract)

My walk-away floor is set deliberately low as a **screening permission**, not an
expectation: do **not** auto-reject mission-aligned / priority roles for being
below market — score them on mission, then I'll negotiate.

**Negotiation scripts** (use when drafting follow-ups and offer responses):

> "Mission alignment is why I'm here, not what I'm paid in. Underpaying senior
> talent isn't impact — it's churn. Let's build a package that reflects market
> for the role."

> "(For grade-governed roles) I understand comp is governed by the [grade] grid.
> I'm asking that the package reflect the appropriate step within the grade
> based on my experience and relevant delivery."
